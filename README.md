📊 Restaurant Industry Data Analysis
A comprehensive data analysis project that uncovers actionable insights about the restaurant industry using Python and data visualization libraries.

📌 Project Overview
This project explores key attributes of restaurants such as cuisines, pricing, online delivery, table booking services, customer reviews, and geographical distribution. The analysis is designed to help restaurant owners, stakeholders, and enthusiasts understand industry trends and customer preferences.

🎯 Objectives
Uncover trends and patterns in the restaurant industry.

Analyze the relationships between various attributes (cuisines, pricing, services, ratings, and reviews).

Provide actionable insights for business decisions and customer engagement.

🗂️ Dataset
Records: 9,500+ restaurants

Key Attributes:

Restaurant Name

Country & City

Cuisine Types

Price Range (1 = Lowest, 4 = Highest)

Online Delivery & Table Booking Services

Ratings & Reviews

Votes

Geographic Coordinates (Latitude & Longitude)

⚙️ Methodology
Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Handled missing values and inconsistencies.

Normalized data for effective analysis.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Performed detailed analysis on:

Cuisines

Cities

Price range distribution

Online delivery and table booking services

Ratings & reviews

Restaurant chains

Geographic clusters

Statistical Analysis

Correlation between review length & ratings.

Correlation between votes & ratings.

Analysis of cuisine combinations.

Visualization

Built visual insights using Seaborn, Matplotlib, and Folium for maps.

📈 Key Insights
Top 3 most common cuisines identified and their share.

Cities with the highest number of restaurants and average ratings.

Price range distribution and its relation to services like online delivery and table booking.

Online delivery is widely offered, especially by mid-to-high-end restaurants.

Notable negative correlation (-0.5997) between review length and ratings.

Moderate positive correlation (0.313) between votes and ratings.

Popular cuisine combinations and their impact on ratings.

Geographic mapping revealed restaurant clusters in key areas.

Analysis of restaurant chains, popularity, and service offerings.

Text analysis of reviews highlighted common positive and negative keywords.

Libraries: pandas, numpy, seaborn, matplotlib, folium
